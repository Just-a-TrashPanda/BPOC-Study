## 1. Fill-in-the-Blank (Multiple Choice)

**1.1** TCOLE was created by the ______ Legislature through Senate Bill 236, effective August 30, 1965.
A) 58th  B) 59th  C) 65th  D) 88th
**Answer: B) 59th** — *BPOC 4.2*

**1.2** Under Senate Bill 1445 (88th Legislature), the Commission's authorization continues until ______.
A) September 1, 2029  B) September 1, 2031  C) September 1, 2033  D) September 1, 2035
**Answer: B) September 1, 2031** — *BPOC 4.2*

**1.3** Under §217.1, the minimum age to be licensed as a peace officer or public security officer is ______, unless the applicant holds an associate's degree, has 60 semester hours of college credit, or was honorably discharged after at least two years of active service — in which case the minimum drops to 18.
A) 18  B) 19  C) 21  D) 25
**Answer: C) 21** — *§217.1(b)(1)(A)*

**1.4** Under §218.3, each peace officer must complete at least ______ hours of continuing education per 24-month training unit, including the applicable legislative update.
A) 20  B) 30  C) 40  D) 50
**Answer: C) 40** — *§218.3(b)(1)*

**1.5** Under §218.9, the minimum passing score for the annual firearms proficiency course of fire is ______ for each firearm.
A) 60  B) 70  C) 80  D) 90
**Answer: B) 70** — *§218.9(d)*

**1.6** Under §223.15, the license of a person charged with a felony and placed on community supervision shall be suspended for ______ years.
A) 10  B) 20  C) 30  D) life
**Answer: C) 30** — *§223.15(b)*

**1.7** Under §217.7, when a licensee separates from an agency, the agency must submit a Separation Report (Form F-5) to the commission within ______ business days.
A) 3  B) 5  C) 7  D) 10
**Answer: C) 7** — *§217.7(b)(1)*

**1.8** Under §219.1, an eligible examinee gets ______ attempts to pass a licensing exam, all of which must fall within 180 days of completing the licensing course.
A) 2  B) 3  C) 4  D) unlimited
**Answer: B) 3** — *§219.1(d)*

## 2. Regular Multiple Choice

**2.1** Which statute empowers TCOLE to create rules for its own administration?
A) Chapter 1701, Occupations Code
B) Chapter 2001, Government Code
C) Chapter 37, Texas Administrative Code
D) Chapter 217, Texas Administrative Code
**Answer: A) Chapter 1701, Occupations Code** — *BPOC 4.2*

**2.2** Under §217.1(b), which of the following is *not* one of the Commission's minimum standards for enrollment and initial licensure?
A) Fingerprinting and a search of local, state, and national criminal records
B) Never having been convicted of an offense above a Class B misdemeanor (or a Class B misdemeanor within the last 10 years)
C) Holding a bachelor's degree from an accredited university
D) Passing a psychological examination showing satisfactory psychological and emotional health
**Answer: C) Holding a bachelor's degree from an accredited university** — *§217.1(b); no bachelor's-degree requirement exists — the actual educational floor is a GED or high school diploma (or, for academy enrollment only, an honorable discharge after 24+ months of active duty)*

**2.3** Under §217.5, the commission may deny a license application for which of the following reasons?
A) The applicant is subject to pending administrative action against a commission-issued license
B) The applicant has more than 40 hours of continuing education
C) The applicant's agency is larger than 50 officers
D) The applicant was licensed more than 10 years ago
**Answer: A) The applicant is subject to pending administrative action against a commission-issued license** — *§217.5(a)(9)*

**2.4** Under §223.19, which of the following results in **mandatory** ("shall be revoked") license revocation, rather than discretionary ("may be revoked")?
A) An offense committed against a vulnerable person
B) An offense involving cruelty to animals
C) A felony conviction
D) An offense directly related to the duties of the person's office
**Answer: C) A felony conviction** — *§223.19(a): "The license of a person convicted of a felony shall be immediately revoked." (Options A, B, and D are all "may be revoked" — discretionary)*

**2.5** Under §229.7, which of the following officer deaths would be **excluded** from the Texas Peace Officers' Memorial Monument?
A) Death from a specific medical response to a violation of law encountered on duty
B) Death caused during the officer's own commission of a crime
C) Death from a felonious assault motivated by the officer's status
D) Death authorized for survivor benefits under Government Code Chapter 615
**Answer: B) Death caused during the officer's own commission of a crime** — *§229.7(b)(1)*

**2.6** Under §211.28, when must a law enforcement agency report a licensee's arrest for an offense above a Class C misdemeanor to TCOLE?
A) Within 7 calendar days
B) Within 30 business days
C) Within 30 calendar days
D) Immediately, before the end of the officer's shift
**Answer: B) Within 30 business days** — *§211.28(a)*

**2.7 (scenario)** Officer Williams earned 38 hours of continuing education, including the legislative update, during the first 24-month unit of his training cycle. He plans to make up the shortfall by completing 42 hours in the second unit of the same 48-month cycle. Is this acceptable under §218.3?
A) Yes — as long as the 4-year cycle totals 80 hours, unit-by-unit minimums don't matter
B) No — the 40-hour minimum applies separately to *each* 24-month training unit, so the first unit is short regardless of the second unit's total
C) Yes — deficits automatically roll over with no penalty
D) No — continuing education can only be reported at the end of a full training cycle
**Answer: B)** — *§218.3(a)–(b): the 40-hour requirement is stated per training unit, not averaged across the cycle. (See BPOC 4.5 lecture scenario.)*

## 3. True / False

**3.1** TCOLE's Part 7 rules are organized under Title 37 of the Texas Administrative Code, Chapters 211–229.
**Answer: True** — *BPOC 4.3*

**3.2** Under §217.1, the minimum age to be licensed as a county jailer or telecommunicator is 21.
**Answer: False** — *§217.1(b)(1)(B): the minimum age for jailers and telecommunicators is 18, not 21 (21 is the peace-officer/public-security-officer floor).*

**3.3** Under §218.7, the executive director may waive legislatively required continuing education only where the licensee shows mitigating circumstances — specifically a catastrophic illness/injury lasting more than 12 months, or active military duty exceeding 12 months.
**Answer: True** — *§218.7(a)–(b)*

**3.4** Under §223.13, surrendering one commission-issued license only affects that specific license — any other licenses the person holds remain valid.
**Answer: False** — *§223.13(d): "The surrender of one commission-issued license operates as a surrender of all commission-issued licenses."*

**3.5** Under §221.1, a TCOLE proficiency certificate is required before an officer can be considered proficient or trained in a given area.
**Answer: False** — *§221.1(a): certificates are "neither required nor a prerequisite for establishing proficiency or training."*

**3.6** Under §219.1, if a licensing candidate is not appointed within two years of passing the licensing exam, the license is placed in inactive status.
**Answer: True** — *§219.1(g)*

**3.7** Under §218.9, the minimum annual handgun qualification course of fire is 50 rounds, with at least 20 of those rounds fired at or beyond seven yards, including one timed reload.
**Answer: True** — *§218.9(c)(1)*

## 4. Multiple Choice — Definitions

**4.1** What does the **L2** form represent in the appointment/reactivation process?
A) Statement of appointment
B) Declaration of the lack of any drug dependency or illegal drug use
C) Declaration of psychological and emotional health
D) Separation of appointment
**Answer: B)** — *BPOC 4.4 (L1–L3, F5 review); §217.7(a)(10)*

**4.2** What does the **L3** form represent?
A) Statement of appointment
B) Declaration of the lack of any drug dependency or illegal drug use
C) Declaration of psychological and emotional health
D) Separation of appointment
**Answer: C)** — *BPOC 4.4; §217.7(a)(10)(A)(i)*

**4.3** What is the **F-5 (or F5R)**?
A) Statement of appointment
B) Declaration of psychological and emotional health
C) A form confirming continuing-education compliance
D) The Separation of Appointment report / return
**Answer: D)** — *BPOC 4.4; §217.7(b)(1), §217.7(a)(8)*

**4.4** Under §217.7, what does **PHS** stand for, and what is it used for?
A) Personal History Statement — a notarized statement obtained before appointing a licensee
B) Public Hearing Summary — a record of a contested-case hearing
C) Peace Officer Health Screening — the medical exam required for licensure
D) Prior History Search — a background-check request form
**Answer: A)** — *§217.7(a)(3)*

**4.5** Under §217.8, what is **SOAH**, and when does it get involved in a TCOLE matter?
A) The Texas Supreme Court, which hears final appeals of license revocations
B) The State Office of Administrative Hearings, to which the executive director refers a petition contesting an employment termination report
C) The Sheriffs' Officer Advisory Hearing board, which reviews arrest reports
D) A TCOLE internal division that issues proficiency certificates
**Answer: B)** — *§217.8(c)*

**4.6** Under §218.3, what is the difference between a "training unit" and a "training cycle"?
A) A training unit is 4 years and a training cycle is 2 years
B) A training unit is 2 years and a training cycle is 4 years
C) They are the same length — 2 years each
D) A training unit is 1 year and a training cycle is 2 years
**Answer: B)** — *§218.3(b)–(c); BPOC 4.5*

**4.7** What is a **PSR**, as referenced in §217.7's pre-appointment checklist?
A) Personal Status Report — obtained from the commission before appointing a licensee
B) Public Safety Record — a licensee's driving history
C) Proficiency Status Rating — a licensee's firearms qualification score
D) Peace Officer Standards Report — an annual TCOLE audit document
**Answer: A)** — *§217.7(a)(2)*

## A note on one thing I couldn't fully verify

The BPOC lecture scenario for §211.27/.28 (Officer Jones's DWI arrest) states the answer is that **three parties** must report an arrest to TCOLE within 30 days: the arresting agency, the officer's employer, and the officer who was arrested. I could only find clear rule-text support for the **agency's** reporting duty (§211.28 — the arresting/employing agency reports within 30 business days). §211.27 covers an individual's duty to report *name/address changes and dishonorable discharges*, not arrests specifically, and I didn't find a TCOLE-rule citation requiring the arrested officer personally to self-report the arrest within the same window. This might be covered elsewhere (Occupations Code §1701.-something, or it may be instructor-added practical guidance beyond the bare rule text) — worth checking with your instructor before I write a quiz question asserting it as rule-text fact.
