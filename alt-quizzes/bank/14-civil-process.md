# Chapter 14: Civil Process — Question Bank

*This bank covers BPOC Chapter 14 (LOs 14.1–14.6): civil liability exposure for officers and
agencies, the differences between civil and criminal law, contempt of court, the three sources
of authority that create Texas courts, general court jurisdiction, and the vocabulary and steps
of a civil action. This chapter leans heavily on specific statutory citations (Texas Civil
Practice and Remedies Code, Local Government Code, Government Code) and definitional vocabulary
(plaintiff, defendant, citation, default judgment, answer, judgment) — expect a lot of straight
recall and definition-matching, with a few scenario/comparison questions layered in.*

### Q1 — Recall

improper act. What are they?

**Explanation:** BPOC 14.1 lists three separate liability tiers: the level of government
