### Q21 — Scenario
**LO:** 4.4 · **Source:** §217.7(a)(7), §217.7(a)(10)(A)

- [ ] A copy of his DD-214

### Q45 — Scenario
**LO:** 4.9 · **Source:** §211.28(a); §211.27(a)

Officer Jones was arrested for DWI by a different agency while off duty in another town. Under
the rules covered in this chapter, whose responsibility is it to report the arrest to TCOLE?

- [ ] Only Officer Jones himself — arrests are self-reported like name and address changes
- [ ] Only the arresting agency — Jones's own department has no independent duty
- [x] Any agency that receives information of the arrest has its own duty to report it under §211.28 — Jones's employer can't assume the arresting agency already handled it
- [ ] No one; TCOLE only tracks convictions, not arrests

**Explanation:** §211.28(a) puts the reporting duty on "an agency" that "receives information"
of the arrest — that can be more than one agency independently, and neither can assume the
other already reported it. Note: §211.27 (the individual's own 30-day reporting duty) covers
name changes, address changes, and dishonorable discharges — it does not, in the rule text
reviewed here, establish a personal duty for Jones to self-report his own arrest. If your
instructor's answer key says otherwise, that's likely drawing on a statute section not covered
in this bank yet (check Occupations Code §1701.x) — worth confirming before treating it as
settled.
