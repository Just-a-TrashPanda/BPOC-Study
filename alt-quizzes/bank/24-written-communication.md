# Chapter 24: Written Communication — Question Bank

*This bank covers BPOC Chapter 24 (LOs 24.1–24.15): parts of speech, complete sentences,
sentence-clarity problems, punctuation, field notes, observation/descriptive skills, field
notebook content, the 5W1H reporting questions, police report uses and criteria, report types,
report organization, and confidentiality of sex offense victims. Per the emphasis requested,
coverage of basic grammar (24.1–24.2) and punctuation marks (24.4) is light — a handful of
questions to confirm the fundamentals — while sentence-clarity problems (24.3) and everything
from field notebook content and report-writing mechanics onward (24.7–24.15) get the deepest
coverage, since that's where the exam-relevant lists and details live.*
