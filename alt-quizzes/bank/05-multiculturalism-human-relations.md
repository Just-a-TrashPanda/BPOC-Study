# Chapter 5: Multiculturalism and Human Relations — Question Bank

*This bank covers BPOC Chapter 5 (LOs 5.1–5.8): the definitions and origins of prejudice, forms
and targets of prejudice, the functions of discrimination, the importance of impartial
enforcement, sensitivity to others' feelings and needs, public perceptions of peace officers,
and cross-cultural communication. This chapter is definition- and list-heavy, so expect a lot of
straight recall and definition-matching, plus a handful of scenario questions that ask you to
apply a listed concept (a function of prejudice, a form of prejudice, an impartiality principle)
to a short fact pattern.*

### Q1 — Definition
**LO:** 5.1 · **Source:** BPOC 5.1

BPOC defines **attitude** as:

- [x] A cognitive position based on a person's knowledge, feelings, and experiences about someone or something, influencing them to behave in a certain way toward that person or thing
- [ ] A fixed, unchangeable trait a person is born with
- [ ] Acting on the basis of prejudice
- [ ] Shared culture and background among a group of people

**Explanation:** BPOC 5.1: attitude is a cognitive position built from knowledge, feelings, and
experience that shapes behavior toward a person or thing — and it can change as life experience
changes.
